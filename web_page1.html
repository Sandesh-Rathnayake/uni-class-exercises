NSBM Green University

Officially established in 2011, our university has been committed to providing high-quality,
globally recognized education to meet the growing needs of Sri Lanka’s higher education sector.
In 2016, our journey reached a significant milestone with the opening of our state-of-the-art
university town in Homagama, located in Colombo Suburbs, which marked a new era for Sri Lankan education.
This purpose-built campus is designed to offer a modern, student-friendly environment that supports academic
excellence, innovation, and sustainable living.

            Operating as a self-financed university, NSBM is renowned for its world-class academic offerings with the intention of providing an opportunity for
            both national and international students to have a fully-fledged education in Sri Lanka. Our state-of-the-art university offers highly recognized
            undergraduate and postgraduate programmes offered locally or in affiliation with top-ranked foreign universities under five faculties: Business,
            Computing, Engineering, Science, and Postgraduate Studies.

The unique model of our university, underpinned by a steadfast commitment to excellence, has received widespread recognition through numerous awards and accolades since its inception.
